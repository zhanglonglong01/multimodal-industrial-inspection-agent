# Synthetic Centrifugal Pump Maintenance Manual

> Demo document only. It is not a manufacturer manual and must not be used for real maintenance.

## Normal operating observations

For the `PUMP-001` demo asset, stable outlet pressure and low vibration are the expected baseline. Trend changes should be compared with the recorded operating state rather than interpreted from a single point.

## Seal leakage indicators

A visible liquid trace near the seal area, increasing vibration, and falling outlet pressure form a stronger inspection pattern than any one observation alone. The synthetic scenario uses this combination to represent `pump_seal_leakage`.

## Demo inspection response

Record the leak location, verify that the liquid trace is not residual cleaning fluid, compare pressure and vibration over the same time window, and prepare the equipment for review by a qualified maintainer. This document does not authorize operating or shutdown actions.
