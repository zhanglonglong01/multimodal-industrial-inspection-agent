# Synthetic Induction Motor Maintenance Manual

> Demo document only. It is not a manufacturer manual and must not be used for real maintenance.

## Normal operating observations

For the `MOTOR-001` demo asset, bearing temperature, vibration, and current should be considered together. A current value inside its normal band does not rule out a localized mechanical bearing problem.

## Bearing fault indicators

Concurrent increases in bearing vibration and bearing temperature, especially when visually localized near the bearing housing, are represented in the demo as `motor_bearing_fault` evidence. The label is synthetic ground truth, not a validated diagnosis rule for real equipment.

## Demo inspection response

Preserve the measurement window, confirm sensor units, document visible discoloration, and request review by a qualified maintainer before deciding on intervention.
