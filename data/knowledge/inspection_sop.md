# Synthetic Visual and Sensor Inspection SOP

> Demo procedure only. It does not replace site safety rules or qualified engineering judgment.

## Evidence capture

1. Confirm the selected asset identifier.
2. Record the image fixture or uploaded image reference.
3. Use the same observation window for related sensor trends.
4. Keep observed facts separate from inferred failure causes.
5. Mark missing or low-quality evidence explicitly.

## Evidence handling

An image label, a sensor anomaly window, and a knowledge document section are separate evidence items. Later diagnosis stages may relate them, but must not rewrite inferred causes as direct observations.

## Normal scenario

When the image has no highlighted defect and all synthetic signals remain inside their baseline ranges, record the inspection as a normal control case. Do not invent a failure mode to force a maintenance action.
